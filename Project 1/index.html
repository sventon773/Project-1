Welcome!

Burlington, Vermont is surrounded by some of the best ski mountians in the North East. Wether you already have a pass such as the Ikon or Epic Pass or just looking for cheap local day tickets, this guide helps students at UVM find the best spots to ski or snowboard

Mountian Overview

Here are some of the top mountian destinations closest to campus:

  • Bolton Vallet: This Mountian is closet to campus, and well know for night skiing!
  • Stowe Mountian Resort: This Mountian has very good terrain and very accesible via College Street bus routes/carpools.
  • Sugarbush Resort: This Mountian has great variety, deep woods, and a very good culture.
  • Smugglers' Notch: The Family owned moutian has many peaks, and alot of glades.
