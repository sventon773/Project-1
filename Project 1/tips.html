Adive for Riding from UVM

1. Join the UVM Ski and Snowboard Club

UVM SSC is one of the largest student clubs on campus. Joining this club will give you access to a bunch of discounted season passes, bus rides to local mountians, and many events throughout the year.

2. Carpooling and Rides

If you dont have a car, dont worry. There are many ways to connect with other students to find rides.
